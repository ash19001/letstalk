# Let's Talk, Rochdale — research notes

Compiled 5 November 2026 from public sources. Facts the owner must confirm are marked **confirm**.

## Who they are

- **Name on the sign:** Let's Talk — Wellbeing Centre, 74 Drake Street, Rochdale OL16 1PQ, 07930 606 926.
- **Legal entity (past):** LET'S TALK FIND YOUR VOICE COMMUNITY INTEREST COMPANY, no. 12129881, incorporated 30 July 2019, registered at a private address in Rochdale (on the Companies House record; not repeated here because this repository is public). Humera Haqqani was the controlling director; a second director resigned on 18 July 2025. **Voluntarily dissolved 14 October 2025.** Source: Companies House, https://find-and-update.company-information.service.gov.uk/company/12129881
- **Not a registered charity.** No Charity Commission entry for any Rochdale "Let's Talk". The website therefore says "funded by one family" and "community organisation", never "charity" or "CIC". **confirm** what entity runs the centre now.
- **Founder:** Humera Haqqani MBE (June 2022: "Managing Director, Let's Talk. For services to Business and to the community in Rochdale"). Moved to Rochdale from the Netherlands in 1999; Urdu community-radio presenter; driving instructor who gave free lessons to domestic-violence survivors. Quote on the MBE: "I'm just doing my duty as a human being." Sources: MCB honours list; Rochdale Online 1 June 2022 (via Wayback).
- **Income scale (FY to 31 July 2024):** turnover £2,741, loss £3,052, net assets £1,091, with money owed to the founder. Consistent with "family-funded".
- **Languages:** English, Urdu, Punjabi (Rochdale wellbeing directory).

## The premises

- 74 Drake Street was Pennine Photographic & Binocular Centre (camera shop, est. 1987), closed on the owner's retirement. Leasehold sold for £85,000 on 8 July 2019 (HM Land Registry price paid).
- **Homes Under the Hammer:** Series 23 episode 67 (BBC One, 28 January 2020) covers "a commercial space in Rochdale" whose buyers planned "creating something for the community"; Series 25 episode 36 (9 August 2022) returns "to Rochdale to see a commercial building Martel first visited back in 2019". The address is never named on screen, so the identification is strong but inferential. **confirm** with the owner (episode numbers, what was said).
- Council lists it as a non-designated heritage asset ("74 Drake Street, Commercial").

## History worth telling

- 2019: founded; opened with an enrolment day in January 2020 (English for beginners, confidence, money management).
- 2020–22 programme on the old site: Monday Ladies Club 11am–2pm (health talks in Urdu with GPs, Bollywood dance, yoga, self-defence, Urdu poetry with University of Manchester volunteers); ESOL and GCSE English; breakfast club and coffee mornings; art relaxation; domestic-violence survivor workshops; monthly women's health clinic with a female doctor; free health-check Fridays; computer classes; Friday mental-health workshop with a consultant psychiatrist; men's "Rohni Mehfil" with dinner.
- 22 December 2022: Christmas lunch for the homeless and people in need (Rochdale Online).
- 12 January 2023: free lunch, food packs, soft toys and warm clothes (Rochdale Online). Thursday soup kitchen listed as "every Thursday 12.30pm to 2pm" from December 2022.
- 6 June 2022: "Let's Talk Zindabad" at Contact Theatre, about 15 women performing poetry, song and storytelling (University of Manchester).
- 2023: Royal Exchange Theatre community partner; "The Big Dream" exhibition (3 April–26 November 2023). Royal Exchange (verbatim, checked 10 Nov 2026): "At their core they operate on friendship, trust and love, spreading joy and positivity." and, separately, "Let’s Talk is a community organisation for Women in the heart of Rochdale which aims to give confidence back to the community" (do not splice them). Sadia, "Rochdale Community Connecter for The Royal Exchange Theatre": "Let’s Talk are a one in a lifetime community."
- 14 June 2024: Embassy of Utopia, Manchester City of Literature's Festival of Libraries: women wrote and performed in English, Urdu and Punjabi.
- 2 September 2024: Know Your Numbers Week blood-pressure checks at the centre with Rochdale Council and Living Well.
- Hosts other groups: Soul Sisters (domestic-abuse survivors' collective) women's group Mon and Tue 9.45am–12pm (Action Together listing) — **confirm** if still running; REIGN art nights (March 2024).

## The rota as the owner described it (November 2026)

- Monday: women's session, 30–40 women; one hour for them, one hour of teaching (mental, physical, exercise); food served.
- Wednesday morning: English class, anyone welcome. Afternoon: men's club with refreshments; a psychologist and a psychiatrist attend.
- Thursday: community lunch for anyone in need; tea, coffee, cookies, warm lunch, free food items and clothes; cost-of-living packages (oil, rice, flour) tailored to the person.
- Friday, Saturday: closed. (Tuesday not mentioned: treated as closed. **confirm**.)
- Sunday: meditation club for men.
