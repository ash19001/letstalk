---
title: Our women on stage at the Festival of Libraries
date: 2024-06-14
summary: Women from Let’s Talk wrote and performed in English, Urdu and Punjabi for Manchester City of Literature’s Embassy of Utopia. For some, it was their first time on a stage.
---

After writing workshops at the centre with Urussa Malik and Amina Beg, women from Let’s Talk performed their own words at the Embassy of Utopia, part of Manchester City of Literature’s Festival of Libraries.

They wrote in English, Urdu and Punjabi about the world they want: “I want there to be peace in the world.” “To be happy, peaceful, no fighting with relatives.”

For some, it was their first time on a stage. We are very proud of every one of them.

As reported by [Manchester City of Literature](https://www.manchestercityofliterature.com/news/lets-talk-rochdale-on-embassy-of-utopia/), July 2024.
