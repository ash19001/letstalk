---
title: Free lunch, food packs and warm clothes
date: 2023-01-12
summary: Thursday lunch with food packs, soft toys and warm clothing to take home, for anyone homeless or struggling in the cold.
---

On Thursday 12 January a team of volunteers served a free lunch and handed out food packs, soft toys and warm clothing from the centre on Drake Street.

One volunteer said: “People devoured the lunch and were delighted to receive the food packs, soft toys and warm clothing.”

The Thursday lunch runs every week. Come in, there is no referral and nothing to pay.

As reported by Rochdale Online, 17 January 2023.
