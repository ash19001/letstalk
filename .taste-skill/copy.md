# Let's Talk — copy deck (working notes, not shipped)

## Inventory (what only this client has)
1. The name: "Let's Talk", first person plural, an invitation.
2. The sign: orange fascia, black letters, "Talk" inside a white speech bubble with a tail.
3. 74 Drake Street, Rochdale OL16 1PQ: a former camera shop (Pennine Photographic, est. 1987) with grey roller shutters, bought at auction in July 2019, filmed for Homes Under the Hammer.
4. The week: Monday women, Wednesday English then the men's club, Thursday lunch, Sunday men's meditation. Closed Tuesday, Friday, Saturday.
5. "One hour for them, one hour to teach them": healthy mind, body, soul.
6. The food package: oil, rice, flour, made up for the person and what they eat.
7. Thursday: tea, coffee, cookies, a warm lunch, free clothes.
8. A psychologist and a psychiatrist in the room on Wednesday afternoons.
9. 2019: one mental-health workshop that grew.
10. Funded by one family. No grant to lose, no referral to need.
11. English, Urdu and Punjabi spoken.
12. Rochdale: the town that invented the co-op (Toad Lane, 1844), Gracie Fields, the Town Hall clock, Drake Street running from the station to the centre.

## The idea
On this page, **the white speech bubble around "Talk" on the Drake Street sign** becomes **the one shape that holds everything: the giant orange field that says what's on today, the frame around every photograph, the button, and the mark beside each day of the week.**
Competitor test: Rochdale Mind at 3-11 Drake Street has no bubble, needs a referral form and a welcome appointment. The sentence fails for them. Good.

## The live thing
The hero bubble speaks the real rota for the visitor's clock in Rochdale: "It's Thursday. Community lunch is on now. 12.30pm till 2pm. Come in." Without script it lists the week in short. Works on a phone with a thumb (it is text). One thing, not a playground.

## The argument (claim → proof)
1. Let's Talk is a free place to talk, eat and learn, in a shop on Drake Street; you can just come in. → the sign's bubble, enormous, saying what's on today; the address; "When to come".
2. Every week has the same shape. → the rota, all seven days, with times (placeholders flagged).
3. It's more than talking: food, clothes, English, exercise, a psychologist. → "What happens here", twelve short points beside one big shaped photograph.
4. This is our corner of Rochdale. → one frame whose picture changes: Drake Street, the station, Toad Lane, the Town Hall; later the centre's own photographs.
5. People's lives change here, and partners say so. → stories (samples until the owner supplies real ones) + three real, sourced partner quotes.
6. Here's what's happening now. → the latest announcements, filed like folders.
7. One family funds this; you can help with food, clothes or an hour. → "Bring what you can", the bubble returns, phone number.
8. Keep this. → a coupon to cut out: address, days, phone, directions.
9. The name, large, and the practical lines. → footer with the mark built from flat shapes, credits, Samaritans line.
