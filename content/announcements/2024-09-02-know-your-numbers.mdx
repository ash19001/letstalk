---
title: Free blood pressure checks at the centre
date: 2024-09-02
summary: Rochdale Borough Council and Living Well came to Drake Street for Know Your Numbers Week, with free blood pressure checks and lifestyle advice.
---

On Monday 2 September, 11.30am to 12.30pm, the council's public health team and Living Well were at the centre for Know Your Numbers Week.

Anyone could walk in for a free blood pressure check and a chat about healthy living. No appointment needed.
